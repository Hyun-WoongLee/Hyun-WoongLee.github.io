About

Studying learning alongside AI

In the society of the future, AI will be even more capable than it is today. Yet however far it advances, AI cannot replace people, and people cannot simply depend on it. As learners co-evolve with AI, they must learn to coexist and collaborate with it. My research addresses this through two topics.

The first is critical AI literacy. To collaborate well with AI, learners must be able to judge which parts of the information AI provides are factual and trust it only as far as the evidence allows, rather than accepting it without question. I examine whether people accept information from humans and from AI in the same way and, if they do not, how and why their responses differ. To find out, I gather several kinds of evidence, including brain activation.

The second is self-regulation in digital learning environments. Discerning the facts in AI-generated information is not the end of the story. To keep learning with AI, learners must decide how far to rely on AI and digital devices, and must regulate the negative emotions that AI hallucinations and errors can trigger. I study how to support this self-regulation, including how learners should perceive AI, so that they can prevent AI dependency and digital addiction and achieve self-directed learning with AI and digital well-being.

Research projects

2025

Project manager

Developing Research Method Guidelines and Cases for Multimodal Data in the Learning Sciences

This project developed concrete guidelines and research cases for systematically collecting and effectively analyzing multimodal data with learning sciences research equipment. It aimed to encourage interest in learning sciences research and the effective use of research equipment.

2024

Project manager

Defining Competencies and Indicators for Analyzing an AI Teaching and Learning Platform

This project aimed to derive competencies for learning analytics, develop metrics for the learning activities that occur on an AI teaching and learning platform, check that the research findings were incorporated, and evaluate the dashboard that presents learning outcomes.

2024

Project manager

Developing a New Course, Veritas Course 1: Self and Community Through Learning

Learning spans many domains and levels, so understanding it requires an interdisciplinary approach. The course Scientific Understanding of Learning is organized around five content domains (understanding learning, cognitive activity, emotions in learning, learners, and learning innovation) and three levels of learning (brain, individual, and community). The content domains set the weekly lecture topics, and three instructors divide the three levels among themselves. Through collaborative team teaching, each weekly topic is taught at all three levels, which fosters interdisciplinary understanding and in-depth discussion.

2024

Project manager

Investigating AI Tools and Teaching Cases for Hyper-Personalized Learning

This project surveyed AI tools that support learner-centered, hyper-personalized learning and analyzed how these tools are used in university classrooms. Drawing on activity theory, it also analyzed the contradictions among system elements that arise when university instructors bring AI tools into their courses, and proposed solutions.

2024

Research assistant

Learning and Development Research Using Multimodal Data (Phase 1)

Collecting multimodal data on learners’ cognitive, affective, and physical development over time makes it possible to study developmental processes scientifically and to analyze the many factors that influence learning. For example, home and school environments can affect learners’ brain development, widening educational gaps and contributing to underachievement. The longitudinal data also make it possible to analyze how the age at which learners use digital technology, and how often they use it, affect literacy.

2024

Project manager

Establishing a Development Plan for the Learning Sciences Research Institute

This study is significant in that it set out the vision and development directions for the first learning sciences research institute established in Korea. It sought concrete strategies for the institute to grow into a world-class research institution that leads future education on the basis of a scientific understanding of learning and practical prescriptions. It is hoped that the proposed plan will be implemented and bring about educational innovation in which every learner’s potential is fully realized.

2023

Principal investigator

Research Project Awarded by NAVER Connect Foundation (Independent Research)

This project analyzed discussion interaction data collected through Slack while learners studied on an AI learning platform, and proposed effective online learning strategies.

2023

Research assistant

A Systematic Integration Plan for the Efficient Use of Online Content at Seoul National University

To develop interdisciplinary talent who will shape the future and contribute to society, Seoul National University needs to build an AI-based online education system and strengthen personalized education. This study proposed ways to develop online content systematically and manage it efficiently. It (1) reviewed exemplary online education cases in Korea and abroad, (2) identified the online content held by various units, and (3) analyzed the needs of instructors, learners, and staff regarding content development, use, and management. Based on these results, it (4) proposed improvements for integrating and using online content.

2022

Research assistant

Learning Effects of AI-Based English Education Apps

This study examined the effectiveness of AI-based English education apps in online and face-to-face learning environments and explored learning strategies for using these apps.
